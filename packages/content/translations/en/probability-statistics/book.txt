Probability & Statistics
From uncertainty to evidence-based decisions
Data and sampling, events and distributions, random variables, joint models, sampling limits, estimation, testing, regression, and Bayesian updating. Every lesson includes derivations, worked examples, exercise solutions, and local interactive demonstrations.
Learners with elementary algebra; continuous probability requires single-variable integration, and joint densities use basic double integrals.
A complete introductory course in ten chapters and fifty lessons. The first half develops probability and sampling models; the second develops frequentist inference, basic linear models, and Beta–binomial Bayesian inference. Measure-theoretic probability, advanced stochastic processes, multiple regression, comprehensive experimental design, and advanced nonparametric methods belong to later courses.
