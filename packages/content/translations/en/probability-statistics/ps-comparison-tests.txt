Means differ by one
Choose assignment $2$ and count assignments with absolute mean difference at least $1$.
The groups are $\{1,3\}$ and $\{2,4\}$, with mean difference $-1$. Four of six assignments have absolute difference at least $1$, so two-sided $p=4/6=2/3$.
