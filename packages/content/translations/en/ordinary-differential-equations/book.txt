Ordinary Differential Equations
From local change to complete motion
First-order equations, existence and uniqueness, linear and nonlinear systems, damping and resonance, Laplace transforms, series, and numerical methods. Explanations, derivations, worked examples, exercises, and interactive figures form a complete introductory course.
For readers with single-variable calculus. Linear systems use matrices and eigenvalues, with prerequisite links to Linear Algebra
Eleven chapters and fifty-five lessons cover a complete undergraduate introduction: local theory, second- and higher-order linear equations, Laplace transforms, linear systems, basic nonlinear dynamics, series, and numerical methods. PDEs have a separate volume. Manifold dynamics, full distribution theory, and solver engineering are outside this scope. Fourier expansions and full Sturm-Liouville theory belong to the later PDE volume; here we introduce the simplest boundary eigenvalue problem.
