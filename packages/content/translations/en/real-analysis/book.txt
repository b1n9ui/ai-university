Completeness of the reals, sequences and series, compactness and continuity, differentiation, Riemann integration, function sequences, and limit interchange. Each lesson includes proof strategies, hypotheses, counterexamples, full exercise solutions, and local interactions.
Readers who know single-variable calculus and want to practice proofs; review logic and sets in Discrete Mathematics as needed
A complete first course on the real line in eight chapters and forty lessons. Starting from a complete ordered field, it rigorously develops single-variable calculus and uniform convergence. General metric spaces, measure and Lebesgue integration, functional analysis, and multivariable analysis belong to later courses.
