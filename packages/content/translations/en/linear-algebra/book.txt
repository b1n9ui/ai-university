Linear Algebra
From systems to spaces, projections, and factorizations
A complete finite-dimensional introduction: systems, matrices and maps, vector spaces, the four fundamental subspaces, determinants, eigenvalues, orthogonality, least squares, symmetric matrices, SVD, networks, and data applications.
For readers with elementary algebra and plane geometry; rotations use basic trigonometry, with no calculus prerequisite
Ten chapters and fifty lessons form a complete introductory course in finite-dimensional real vector spaces and matrices, including complex eigenvalues, pseudoinverses, low-rank approximation, conditioning, and PCA. Full Jordan theory, abstract duality, tensors, and infinite-dimensional analysis are left to later volumes.
