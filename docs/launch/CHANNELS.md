## Facts for a human-authored account

- Project: AI University, https://github.com/b1n9ui/ai-university.
- Scope: ten complete books; eight mathematics, Python, and Building Projects with AI. 383 lessons, 778 examples, 1,878 exercises including reviews.
- Languages: separate English and Chinese static sites, with whole-book downloads in each language.
- AI role: offline authorship and revision; no runtime chat/model service.
- Reader interaction: locally calculated diagrams, fixed program traces, worked solutions, exercises, local progress.
- Status: source and releases public; code MIT, original textbook text CC0.
- Limitation: not individually human-reviewed; automated checks are partial evidence, not proof of every statement or measured teaching effectiveness.
- Ask: concrete corrections to claims, examples, solutions, diagram states, or wording.
- Exclusion: pending C contribution is not a published book.

A human author should add only their own true experience, motivation, decisions, and observations. Do not fabricate a personal development story or testimonials.
